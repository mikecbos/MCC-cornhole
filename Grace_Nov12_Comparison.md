### 2. Majors, Academic Fit & Honors Access

Each offer puts Grace on a distinctly different academic and career trajectory within the biological sciences:

#### **Plymouth State University: B.S. Environmental Biology + University Honors Program**



- **Academic Track:** Focused on ecology, field research, conservation, and environmental science—leveraging PSU's White Mountains location.
- **Distinction:** Direct admission into the **University Honors Program** at no additional cost, providing smaller seminar-style classes, priority registration, and mentored independent project work.   
- **Institutional Standing:** Regional public university (ranked #12 in NH; top 35% nationally for Biology programs). Best suited if she prefers outdoor/field biology, environmental policy, or close faculty mentorship in a tight-knit cohort.

#### **Regis College: Biology / Regulatory and Clinical Research Management (BA or BS/MS)**



- **Academic Track:** This is a **specialized, career-targeted dual-degree pathway (BS/MS)**. While freshman year covers foundational biology, the Regulatory and Clinical Research Management (RCRM) track trains students specifically for clinical trials, FDA compliance, quality assurance, and drug/medical device commercialization.   
- **Strategic Location:** Located in Weston, MA (minutes from the Route 128/Cambridge biotech and pharmaceutical corridor), with practicum pipelines into companies like Takeda, Genzyme, and Nova Biomedical.
- **Institutional Standing:** Small private Catholic university (~950 undergrads, 9:1 student-to-faculty ratio) best known for nursing and allied health. If she wants to enter the **biotech/pharma corporate sector** right out of school with an accelerated Master's degree rather than going to med school or doing bench research, this specific major has high industry alignment.   

#### **UMass Dartmouth: General Biology / Pre-Health (College of Arts & Sciences)** *(Inferred from baseline; letter shows general merit offer)*   

- **Academic Track:** Broad, traditional B.S. in Biology within an **R2 Doctoral Research University**. Offers stronger lab infrastructure for molecular/cellular biology, marine biology (via SMAST connections), and pre-med/pre-health prerequisites.
- **Institutional Standing:** Ranked #24 in Massachusetts for General Biology, with stronger research output and graduate school visibility than PSU or Regis. *(Note: She can also inquire about applying to the UMassD Honors College if not already invited, though it carries a small ~$390/yr fee).*

### Summary Assessment: How Each Plays

1. **Best Financial & Honors Value — Plymouth State ($21.9k/yr net):** PSU gave Grace their maximum out-of-state merit stack plus Honors Program entry. Financially, it saves roughly **$30,000 over four years** compared to UMass Dartmouth and **$57,000 over four years** compared to Regis. Its main limitation is narrower career scope if she pivots away from environmental/field biology toward lab medicine or pharma.   
2. **Best Research & Pre-Med / Grad School Platform — UMass Dartmouth ($29.3k/yr net):** As an R2 institution, UMassD carries stronger academic weight for PhD programs, medical/dental school applications, or lab-based biosciences. It is a solid middle-ground cost, though the $7,000 scholarship requires maintaining a strict **3.0 GPA** (a meaningful threshold in weed-out college chemistry and biology sequences).   
3. **Best Niche Career Accelerator — Regis College ($36.1k/yr net):** The **BS/MS in Regulatory and Clinical Research Management** is a standout vocational program in the Boston biotech hub. While it costs the most upfront, finishing in 5 years with an MS and clinical trial management experience leads to significantly higher starting salaries ($70k–$85k+ in regulatory affairs) than a standalone B.S. in Biology.
